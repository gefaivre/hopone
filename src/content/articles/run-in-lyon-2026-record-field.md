The 16th **Run In Lyon**, held on **October 4, 2026**, announced **36,000 participants**, up **3,000** on 2025.

## The field

| Event | Entrants |
|---|---|
| Half marathon | 17,000 |
| Marathon | more than 7,000 (record) |
| 10K and Run Solidaire | rest of the field |

The weekend relies on **1,200 volunteers** over three days. The course starts in Vieux Lyon and finishes at Place Bellecour, and the title sponsor is the insurer **Harmonie Mutuelle**.

## Pricing

| Event | Price |
|---|---|
| Marathon | €75 to €89 |
| 10K | €34 to €45 |
| Run Solidaire | €18, of which €4 goes to AFG Autisme |

Lyon’s marathon costs about half as much as the **Paris Marathon**, where a 2027 bib costs **€169**. That gap matters as French road-race demand keeps rising. Lyon is growing its field at prices below Paris’s, while Paris has moved to a ballot to manage demand.
