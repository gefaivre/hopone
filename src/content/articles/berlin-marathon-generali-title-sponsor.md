One of road running’s best-known naming rights has changed hands. **Generali** becomes the title partner of the Berlin Marathon from October 1, 2026, and the first **Generali Berlin Marathon** takes place on **September 26, 2027**.

## The end of the BMW era

BMW held the naming rights for **16 years**, from October 2010 to September 2026, and its first race as title sponsor was in September 2011. It began with a three-year contract and kept renewing. Before BMW, the race carried the name of the hypermarket chain real,- from 2000 to 2010.

During BMW’s 15 races, the course produced **six world records**:

- Patrick Makau, 2:03:38 (2011)
- Wilson Kipsang, 2:03:23 (2013)
- Dennis Kimetto, 2:02:57 (2014)
- Eliud Kipchoge, 2:01:39 (2018) and 2:01:09 (2022)
- Tigst Assefa, 2:11:53 (2023)

The organizer counts **13 world records** on the course in total, “more than at any other marathon.” That record-breaking reputation is a large part of what a title sponsor pays for.

## What Generali is buying

Neither side disclosed the **length or value** of the contract. Generali is not new to the event family: it has sponsored the Berlin Half Marathon since 2019, as well as the Generali 5K and the mini-marathon. The organizer’s press release gives the scale of the asset:

| Indicator | Figure |
|---|---|
| Athletes, 2026 race weekend | more than 86,000, from 162 nations |
| Spectators along the route | about 1.2 million |
| Value creation for Berlin, 2024 event | about €469 million |
| Marathon field from 2027 | more than 50,000 participants expected |

Media exposure is secured as well: **RTL Deutschland** holds the broadcast rights through 2029. Registration for the 2027 race opened on October 1, 2026.
