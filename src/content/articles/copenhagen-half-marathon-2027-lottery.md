The ballot for the **2027 Copenhagen Half Marathon** closed on September 30 with **188,522 entries** for **45,000 places**. That is about **4.2 applicants per bib** (Hopone calculation). The race takes place on **September 19, 2027**.

## Demand came fast

- **47,320** entries arrived in the first 12 hours.
- More than **100,000** had applied by September 19.
- About **70% of applicants live outside Denmark**. The UK, Germany, Norway and Sweden were the leading markets.

Selected runners will be notified from October 5 and will have **48 hours** to pay.

## Price and revenue

Entry costs **DKK 795 (about $120)** plus a 5% administration fee, or roughly DKK 835 ($126) in total. A full field at the base price represents about **DKK 35.8 million (around $5.4 million)** in entry fees (Hopone calculation: 45,000 × DKK 795).

The organizer, **Sparta Atletik & Løb**, is a nonprofit.

## A pattern across Copenhagen

The marathon is oversubscribed too. The **Copenhagen Marathon 2027** received **80,937 entries for 35,000 places**, or 2.3 applicants per spot. For comparison, the 2026 half marathon, which hosted the World Championships, had 35,000 entrants.

With seven in ten applicants coming from abroad, the race now mostly sells a weekend trip to Copenhagen, with a bib attached.
