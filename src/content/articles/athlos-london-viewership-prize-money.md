**Athlos**, the women-only track and field meet, held its first event outside the United States on **September 25, 2026**, at a sold-out **StoneX Stadium** in London. The organizers report **7.3 million viewers** across their broadcast and streaming partners.

## Where the audience came from

The 7.3 million figure combines several platforms: BBC Red Button, BBC iPlayer and BBC Sport, ION, Bounce, Eurovision, W-Sport, ESPN, X and YouTube. On top of that, the event reports **31.2 million social views** and **48.4 million social impressions**.

These are the organizers’ own numbers, and they add up audiences across platforms with different measurement methods. They are not a single rating comparable to a TV audience figure.

## Prize money as the selling point

The meet’s pitch to athletes is money:

- **$1,057,000** in total prize money
- **$65,000** and a bespoke Tiffany & Co. crown for each event winner
- **40 athletes** across **seven events**

The business model is to pay athletes more and sell a media product built around them. London is the first test of whether that model works outside the US. The early audience numbers give the organizers an argument when they negotiate with sponsors and broadcasters for the next editions.
